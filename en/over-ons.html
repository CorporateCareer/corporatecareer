We know what it feels like not to know where to start. That's why we built the platform we wished we'd had.

Our Mission

Equal Opportunities for Every Ambitious Talent

In the Netherlands, too many students don't know how to break into firms like Goldman Sachs, McKinsey or De Brauw Blackstone Westbroek. Not because they aren't good enough, but simply because they don't know how the process works.

CorporateCareer gives everyone access to the same information and preparation, regardless of who you happen to already know.

Much of that information is fragmented, outdated or hidden behind expensive programmes. We bring it together clearly and for free, so you can make an informed choice yourself.

Our Approach

How We Put Our Information Together

No invented stories. Only information we can back up.

We base ourselves on public sources and our own experience in the sector. Salaries, job titles and firm structures change; we indicate when something is an estimate and update where we can. Found something that isn't right? Let us know.

Verified employer information
We only add a firm or bank to a guide once we can confirm the practice group or sector is actually active in the Netherlands.
Practice areas in depth
Each guide describes what the work involves, the career ladder and the compensation, by practice area and sector.
Continuously updated
Vacancies and company information are checked and updated regularly, so the information stays current.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Honesty over Hype

We write about what the work actually involves, not what sounds good in an advertisement. No empty promises, just concrete information.

Depth over Surface

Our guides go beyond a list of job titles: we explain what the work looks like day to day, from junior to partner.

Concrete over Vague

Our guides give concrete information on tasks, skills and salary ranges, instead of generic career tips.

Equal Access

Whether you study at a university of applied sciences or a research university, everyone gets access to the same information and preparation.
