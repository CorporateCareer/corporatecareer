Data & Analytics Consulting

Discover what data & analytics consulting involves: how consultants help organisations get value from data with analytics, data science and AI, what they do day to day, the career ladder, and how students prepare for an application.

View vacanciesExplore consulting paths

Introduction

What is data & analytics consulting?

Data & analytics consulting is about how an organisation gets value from its data. Consultants help unlock, analyse and turn data into models, dashboards and decisions, from classic business intelligence to data science and AI.

The work combines technology and business: a good model is only valuable if it answers a real question and is actually used. The consultant therefore constantly switches between data, technology and the people who work with the results.

Data & analytics is one of the fastest-growing parts of consulting, partly due to the rise of AI. It appeals to students with a quantitative background who want to stay close to the business.

What it isThe advisory field around getting value from data with analytics, data science and AI.
What it centres onTurning data into models, insights and decisions that move the organisation forward.
Why clients need itTo make better decisions and get the most out of their data and AI.
Why students find it attractiveQuantitative work with direct impact, at the intersection of data and business.

The practice

Three settings where you practise this field

The breakdown below mainly says something about the type of employer and the clients you serve, not about the quality of the work.

Specialist data and AI firmsFirms such as Xebia that focus entirely on data, AI and engineering.
Technology and advisory firms with a data practiceFirms such as Accenture, Capgemini Invent and IG&H with a strong data practice.
Strategy and Big Four firms with an analytics unitStrategy houses and the Big Four with their own analytics and AI teams.

At specialist data and AI firms you work deep in the technology of data and models. Technology and advisory firms often combine data with broader transformation. Strategy and Big Four firms often approach data from strategy or the broader advisory platform. In addition, many data professionals work in-house at a data company.

Building modelsDeveloping and training statistical models and machine learning.
Building dashboards and visualisationsShowing insights clearly so the business can work with them.
Developing AI applicationsUsing AI and generative AI for concrete applications.
Putting models into productionMaking sure a model runs reliably and gets used.
Data platforms and governanceGetting the data foundation in order, from platform to governance.

Step by step

How a data project unfolds

Most data projects follow a similar path, from sharpening the question to a model or dashboard that runs in practice.

Who does which step depends on seniority and role. Junior consultants do much of the gathering, preparation and modelling, while senior consultants set the approach and make the translation to the business.

A large part of the work is in preparing the data: a model is only as good as the data it is built on.

Roles

Roles in the data & analytics practice

Each step up the ladder shifts the emphasis further from building models toward leading teams and managing the client relationship. Most consultants climb this ladder at the same firm, though moving to a data role in the business is common at any level.

Data Analyst / Consultant

The entry role straight after a bachelor's or master's.

Key tasks
Preparing data, analyses and first models, building dashboards.
Core skills
Statistics, programming, precision.
Typical level
Entry-level, after studying.

Senior Consultant / Data Scientist

Independently handles a data part of a project.

Key tasks
Building models, client contact, guiding juniors.
Core skills
Machine learning, communication, business insight.
Typical level
After the first years.

Manager / Lead

Leads a team and the day-to-day running of a data project.

Key tasks
Steering the project, managing the team, dealing with the client daily.
Core skills
Leadership, oversight, client management.
Typical level
Several years of experience.

Senior Manager / Principal

Carries responsibility for several projects and client relationships.

Key tasks
Building client relationships, ensuring quality, winning new projects.
Core skills
Commercial judgement, sector knowledge, network.
Typical level
Senior, on the way to partner.

Partner / Director

Holds ultimate responsibility for clients and the practice.

Key tasks
Client relationships, business development, ultimate responsibility for the firm.
Core skills
Network, track record, entrepreneurship.
Typical level
Most senior.

Career timeline

How long each step takes

Data & analytics consulting has a relatively fast career path. The pace differs by firm and individual, and relates to the projects and results you show.

Many consultants stay a few years and then move to a data role in the business, a data company or a startup. Those who stay progress toward Manager and eventually partner or director.

Depth in a technique such as machine learning or data engineering is valuable in this field and can speed up progression.

LevelTypical time in role
Data Analyst2 to 3 years
Data Scientist2 to 4 years
Manager / Lead2 to 4 years
Partnertop level, 10+ years in total

Compensation

Compensation in data & analytics consulting

Compensation usually consists of a fixed salary that rises with seniority, and at many firms a bonus tied to individual and firm performance. Given the demand for data professionals, pay in this field is generally good.

A day in the life

A typical day for a junior data consultant

The day often starts with a short team stand-up. After that, much of the time goes into preparing data, building or improving a model and making visualisations for the client.

A data consultant constantly switches between the technology and the business: a model is only valuable if it answers a real question. That requires both quantitative work and clear communication.

On a day with a delivery, the team shows the results to the client, explains how the model works, and discusses how the organisation will use it in practice.

Juniors

What junior data consultants do

Junior consultants carry much of the data and modelling work a data project rests on, under the supervision of a Manager or senior.

  • Gathering and combining data
  • Cleaning and preparing data
  • Running exploratory analysis
  • Building and training models
  • Building dashboards and visualisations
  • Validating models
  • Documenting results
  • Supporting the client with usage

Much of this work goes back and forth within the team and with the client before a model is delivered. That repetition is not inefficiency, it is how a junior consultant learns what makes a model reliable and usable in practice.

Compare

Data & Analytics versus other paths

These paths sometimes overlap, which is why students mix them up. The distinction mainly lies in how deep you sit in the data and the technology.

Data & Analytics vs Technology & Digital Consulting

Technology & digital is broader and covers systems and ways of working. Data & analytics focuses specifically on data, models and AI.

Data & Analytics vs Strategy Consulting

Strategy decides an organisation's choices. Data & analytics provides the models and insights that support those choices.

Data & Analytics vs an in-house data scientist

An in-house data scientist works for a single organisation. As a consultant you work for many clients and see many different data problems.

Data consulting vs a data engineer role

A data engineer mainly builds the platforms and pipelines. A data consultant sits closer to the business and the use of the results.

Data & Analytics vs Operations Consulting

Operations improves processes and execution. Data & analytics uses data and models to support that improvement.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is data & analytics consulting?

It is the advisory field around getting value from data: consultants help analyse data and turn it into models, dashboards and decisions, from business intelligence to data science and AI.

What degree do I need?

Most consultants come from a quantitative degree such as econometrics, data science, computer science, mathematics or physics. Programming skill and affinity with data are important.

What does a data consultant do day to day?

Consultants gather and prepare data, build models and dashboards, develop AI applications, and translate the results into decisions for the client.

Do I need to be able to code?

For most roles yes. Python and SQL come up in almost every data project, alongside knowledge of statistics and machine learning.

What is the difference with a data engineer?

A data engineer mainly builds the platforms and pipelines, while a data consultant sits closer to the business and focuses on models, insights and their use.

How much do you earn in data & analytics consulting?

Given the demand for data professionals, pay is generally good. Actual pay varies by firm and is listed where known in the vacancies on this site.

What skills do you need?

Statistics, programming, machine learning, data visualisation, analytical thinking and the ability to explain a technical result clearly.

How do I get into this field as a student?

Build quantitative and programming skills, work on your own data projects, do a relevant internship, and apply via campus or the firm's website.
